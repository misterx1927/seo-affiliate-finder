# Recorded live run (25 November 2026)

`run-7-complete.csv` is the newer run using DataForSEO's optional poisoned-results verification: it contains 100 observations, ten for each of ten queries. Of these, 87 positions are flagged `restored_from_google_web`, while 13 are labelled `original_or_verification_unavailable`; absence of a restoration flag does not confirm that verification was successful. Web-mode replacements can differ from the original Google mobile SERP. There were 69 unique URLs; 21 page analyses were accepted and 48 URLs required review. Among accepted analyses, 15 were labelled relevant, one not relevant, and five uncertain. The label is a lead qualification aid, not a verified placement opportunity. AI outputs and contact details still need independent review.

`run-7-with-recovery.csv` retains the same 100 search positions and includes subsequent analyses for fourteen additional unique pages, raising accepted analyses from 21 to 35 (24 relevant, four not relevant, seven uncertain). The remaining 34 pages have no accepted analysis; the seven uncertain classifications also require human review. After checking page text, several incorrect brand entries and one confirmed duplicate were removed manually; a casino-branded blog was changed to uncertain pending verification of independence. See `../RECOVERY_REVIEW.md`: this is a corrected working snapshot, not an unmodified model response, and remaining names and contacts have not been exhaustively verified. Historical `run_pages` statuses refer to the original run, not the later recovery.

The `analysis_version` column identifies the analyzer used when a page was last processed. Older analyses have not automatically been rerun with the latest prompt; subsequent human corrections do not change this field. An empty version means none was recorded.

`independent-review-2026-11-26.json` contains separate, partial reviews of 26 URLs inspected in independently indexed copies after direct fetches failed. The dashboard can display these records as supplementary checks, clearly distinguished from automated AI results; they do not change the stored database or historical CSV. `run-7-reviewed.csv` combines the original snapshot with these reviews and records the method, date and limitations of each supplementary assessment. The casino.ca contact comes from its public contact page and the listed brands are illustrative, not exhaustive. Indexed content was viewed after the search date and may differ by region or time. This ad hoc review is not a reproducible automated fallback for future runs; see `INDEX_REVIEW.md`.

The `public_contact_candidate` and `contact_candidate_source` columns are separate from the AI contact field. They show a public address found on a same-domain contact page; this is a candidate, not confirmation that the address handles partnerships. In this snapshot, one candidate is a press contact. Address and content were checked after the original search date and may change.

Additional page text was retrieved through DataForSEO Content Parsing after direct connection failures. `casino.org/canada/` remained for review after its AI response failed the brand grounding check; its retrieved text was truncated. Another returned a robot check and another returned a German regional notice; both were rejected after manual inspection and remain for review. The `page_source` column distinguishes provider-retrieved content; the `serp_provenance` column concerns a separate question, the origin of the search position. These pages were retrieved on 26 Nov, after the 25 Nov search: their content may have changed.

These CSV files contain actual Google Canada mobile search results from run 5, rather than fictional demo fixtures. `run-5-complete.csv` has 20 rows from the two searches for which the provider returned a full organic top 10. `run-5-partial.csv` contains 37 rows from the other eight searches, for which the provider returned fewer than ten organic results. Together they contain 57 observations, including URLs that appear under multiple queries.

The AI columns reflect the saved analysis, including a few manually corrected extraction errors. An empty analysis, brand or contact field does not imply that no brands or contact exist. Some pages could not be accessed, and relevance, brands and contact details still need independent verification; see `../QUALITY_REVIEW.md`. No credentials or page text are included. Live databases, API keys and subsequent runs are kept locally and excluded from Git.
